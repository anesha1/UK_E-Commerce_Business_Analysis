# 📊 UK E-Commerce Business Analysis (End-to-End Analytics Project)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an end-to-end business analytics solution built on a UK-based e-commerce dataset.  
The objective was to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights using Python and Power BI.

The analysis focuses on:
- Revenue performance
- Customer behavior
- Product contribution
- Return impact
- Geographic market trends

The final output is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to support strategic and operational decision-making.

---

## 🏭 Industry / Domain
Retail / E-Commerce Analytics

---

## 🎯 Business Problem
The business had large volumes of transactional data but lacked visibility into:
- Which customers and products drive revenue
- How returns affect profitability
- Which markets contribute most to growth
- Where risks and concentration exist

This project converts raw data into insights to guide revenue optimization, risk reduction, and growth strategy.

---

## 📂 Dataset Information
- Source: Public UK E-commerce dataset  
- Size: ~500K+ transaction rows  
- Period Covered: 2010–2011

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- Revenue is highly concentrated in a small number of customers and products.
- The UK dominates overall sales, creating geographic dependency risk.
- Returns represent a small percentage of transactions but cause significant revenue leakage.
- A limited subset of SKUs drives most return losses.
- Year-end seasonality strongly influences revenue growth.

---

## 📈 Business Recommendations

- Reduce dependence on the top market by expanding mid-tier countries.
- Implement targeted monitoring for high-return SKUs.
- Develop retention strategies for top revenue customers.
- Investigate operational drivers of return spikes in peak months.
- Diversify product revenue mix to reduce concentration risk.
